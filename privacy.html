Privacy Policy

Map Explainer Uploader — last updated November 17, 2026

What this app is

Map Explainer Uploader is a private, single-user tool. It is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Chronicles of the Map" to upload that channel's own videos, thumbnails and captions through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube scopes needed to upload and manage videos on the owner's own channel. It does not access, collect or process data of any other user.

Storage and sharing

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
